# Research report

When Nic asks for research, a briefing, or a written report on a topic:

1. Gather 3-5 solid sources with `web_search` and `web_fetch`. Keep the URL for every claim.
2. Write a short report with `write_file` to `report.md` in your workspace: a title, 3-6 crisp
   sections, and a Sources list with the URLs.
3. Deliver it as a file, not a wall of text: end your reply with `[[send: report.md]]` and a
   one-line summary of what you found.

Read `style.md` in this folder for the report style before you write.
